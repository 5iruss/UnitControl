# UnitControl

**UnitControl** is a web-based academic planning system designed to help university students understand, manage, and plan their academic path.

The system transforms traditional university curriculum charts into an interactive academic map, allowing students to track course statuses, understand academic requirements, plan future semesters, and identify potential academic problems.

## Product Goal

UnitControl is designed to answer three simple questions:

1. Where am I in my academic path?
2. What courses can I take next?
3. What academic problems should I be aware of?

## Core Features

- Interactive curriculum map
- Course status management
- Prerequisite and corequisite validation
- Semester planning
- Course recommendations
- Academic warnings and error detection
- Academic progress tracking
- Support for different curricula and entry years
- Admin and support panel
